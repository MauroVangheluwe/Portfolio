For this assignment I designed and built an interactive web story for MoMu, the Fashion Museum in Antwerp, about Walter Van Beirendonck and his label W&LT. The site turns his themes into things you can actually play with: a censorship meter that reveals hidden slogans, and a T-shirt button that shows how mass production empties out meaning. Fashion as a weapon, translated into scroll, motion and interaction with GSAP.

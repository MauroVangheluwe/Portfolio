For this group project, our team of four worked for Abby, a museum in Kortrijk, Belgium. The brief was to create an installation that stimulates visitors and promotes the museum's public creative spaces. Our answer combines a physical installation with NFC tags placed on mirrors: visitors tap a tag with their phone to send in something they made, and their project then appears on the installation in the museum. Other visitors can browse through all the submissions using physical buttons, powered by an Arduino. As the implementer in the team, I helped turn the concept into a working prototype using Canva, Adobe, Visual Studio Code and Arduino.
